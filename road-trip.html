About this map

  • Destination: Revdanda Fort, near Alibaug — where the Kundalika river meets the sea.
  • Live sync: your own dot glides in real time on your screen; everyone else sees your car update roughly every 8 seconds, with a guaranteed refresh at least once a minute. A "Weak signal" pill just means a normal cellular gap — it only turns to "Signal lost" after a few minutes of real silence.
  • Speed & ETA: once you're moving, your card shows your speed and a rough time-to-Revdanda estimate.
  • No login, no lock: anyone who opens this link can see all 4 cars. Please don't forward it beyond the four of you.
  • Keep it in front: we auto-keep your screen awake while you're sharing, and auto-resume the moment you come back if it does pause. But no website — this one included — can share your location with the screen off or the tab switched away; that's an iOS/Android privacy rule, not something any app can bypass. Whoever's phone is sharing needs this tab open and visible.
  • Want truly always-on tracking? For that you need a native app with background permission — WhatsApp's "Share Live Location," Google Maps location sharing, or Life360 all work with the screen locked. Nothing stops you running one of those alongside this map for the fun shared view.

Format & Rules

How ties are broken

Point Difference = Total points scored − Total points conceded across a team's 2 group matches. Highest difference wins the group — regardless of individual match wins.

If two (or more) teams are tied on the exact same point difference, it goes to a sudden-death decider: first team to reach 1 point wins. This page flags it automatically on the Groups section and records the result with one tap.
